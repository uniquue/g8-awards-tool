Rules you can review

The master workbook’s award rules are retained with corrected employee ranges, exact months ÷ 12 proration, and whole-dollar cash reconciliation using the largest fractional remainders. All uploaded employee rows participate.

Scores from 3–5 earn score-based shares; lower scores earn zero. Time off is 40 × score ÷ 5 × time-off percentage × months ÷ 12, rounded to two decimals. Its reference value uses annual salary ÷ 2,080. Hourly salaries in the raw file are annualized using 2,087 hours, matching the source.

QSI removes cash and time-off shares. QSI limits use the full employee count, rounded down. Cash is redistributed across remaining cash shares when scores or selections change. With no cash shares, the pool stays unallocated.

TOA reports include all time off; combined and cash-band reports can overlap. NRB awards are tracked separately. Exported figures are calculated values for review, not an approval or payroll submission.
