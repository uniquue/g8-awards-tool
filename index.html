How each budget line is calculated

Calculations use the employees in your selected directorates. Select all directorates for agency totals. Total salary is the starting amount; it is not an award budget. “Total budget” and “total awards budget” refer to the same initial award pool.

Budget lineCalculationMeaning
Total salaryAdd the annual salaries of all selected employees.The salary base used to calculate funding.
Total budget / total awards budgetTotal salary × award budget rate (default 2.4%).The entire award pool before reserves are deducted.
Supervisory budgetTotal awards budget × supervisory percentage (default 5%).Held separately as a supervisory reserve; not automatically awarded to employees.
NRB budget(Total awards budget − supervisory budget) × NRB percentage (default 10%).NRB is calculated after removing the supervisory reserve.
Rating-based award budgetTotal awards budget − supervisory budget − NRB budget.The remaining funding for rating-based cash awards. The automatic cash pool rounds this amount down to whole dollars.
Cash allocated / remainingAllocated = sum of calculated employee cash awards. Remaining = selected cash pool − allocated.Cash awards depend on scores, award choices, shares, and months. With no eligible cash shares, the pool remains unallocated.
NRB used / remainingUsed = sum of entered employee NRB amounts. Remaining = NRB budget − used.A negative balance means entered NRB awards exceed the NRB budget.

Example using the default percentages

For $1,000,000 in total salary: total awards budget = $1,000,000 × 2.4% = $24,000. Supervisory budget = $24,000 × 5% = $1,200. NRB budget = ($24,000 − $1,200) × 10% = $2,280. Rating-based award budget = $24,000 − $1,200 − $2,280 = $20,520. The three amounts reconcile: $1,200 + $2,280 + $20,520 = $24,000.

All percentages are editable in Budget & allocation settings; this example uses defaults. A manual agency cash pool overrides the automatic rating-based cash pool and is apportioned to selected directorates by salary. It does not change the calculated supervisory or NRB budgets. Grade budgets also use salary shares. Full-workbook exports calculate all directorates together.

Employee award calculations

The master workbook’s award rules are retained with corrected employee ranges, exact months ÷ 12 proration, and whole-dollar cash reconciliation using the largest fractional remainders. All uploaded employee rows participate.

Scores from 3–5 earn score-based shares; lower scores earn zero. Time off is 40 × score ÷ 5 × time-off percentage × months ÷ 12, rounded to two decimals. Its reference value uses annual salary ÷ 2,080. Hourly salaries in the raw file are annualized using 2,087 hours, matching the source.

QSI removes cash and time-off shares. QSI limits use the selected employee count, rounded down; full-workbook exports use the agency count. Cash is redistributed across remaining cash shares when scores or selections change. With no cash shares, the pool stays unallocated.

TOA reports include all time off; combined and cash-band reports can overlap. NRB awards are tracked separately. Exported figures are calculated values for review, not an approval or payroll submission.
